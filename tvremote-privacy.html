TV Remote privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-22

TV Remote: Roku, Samsung, LG ("the app") is published by dt-mobile.

What stays on your device. The app finds TVs on your local Wi-Fi network and talks to them directly over that network. The TV names, addresses, pairing tokens and settings it saves are stored on your device only. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this data.

Voice search. If you use voice search, Android's speech recognition service (provided by Google or your device maker) turns your speech into text; the app only receives the text and sends it to your TV. We never receive or store audio.

Purchases. Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Google Play. Google shares a purchase token with the app so Pro can be unlocked; we do not receive your payment details.

Advertising. Free users can watch optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the advertising ID, coarse location derived from IP address and device information to serve and measure ads. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Pro users are not shown ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13.
