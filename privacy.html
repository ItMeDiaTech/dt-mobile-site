Block Harbor privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

Block Harbor ("the game") is published by dt-mobile.

Data we collect. The game itself stores your score, coins and purchase entitlements on your device only. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this data.

Purchases. In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play. Google shares purchase tokens with the game so rewards can be granted; we do not receive your payment details.

Advertising. The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the advertising ID, coarse location derived from IP address, and device information to serve and measure ads. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and the Harbor Pass subscription or any pass purchase removes interstitial ads.

Children. The game is intended for players aged 13 and over.
